This paper introduces dynamically adaptive deformable feature fusion techniques specifically designed for medical imaging applications. The method addresses the challenge of effectively combining multi-scale features in medical images while adapting to the spatial deformations inherent in biological structures.

## Key Contributions
- Novel deformable feature fusion architecture
- Dynamic adaptation mechanisms for medical imaging
- Improved performance on various medical imaging tasks
- Robust handling of spatial deformations in biological structures

## Applications
The proposed method has been successfully applied to various medical imaging tasks including:
- Hippocampal sclerosis measurement
- Arterial spin labeling asymmetry analysis
- General medical image analysis and segmentation

## Impact
This work contributes to the advancement of computer vision techniques in medical imaging, providing more accurate and robust analysis tools for clinical applications.
