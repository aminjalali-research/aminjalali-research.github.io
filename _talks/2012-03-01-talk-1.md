This talk presents recent advances in developing AI pipelines for epilepsy detection and presurgical decision-making using arterial spin labeling MRI and foundational models for neurological disease prediction.

Key Topics:
* Adaptive metadata-guided supervised contrastive learning
* Arterial spin labeling MRI analysis for epilepsy
* Integration of foundational models in clinical workflows
* Presurgical planning and decision support systems
