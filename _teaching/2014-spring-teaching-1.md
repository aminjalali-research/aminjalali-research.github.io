This advanced graduate course covers the application of deep learning techniques to medical imaging and biomedical signal processing. Students learn to apply state-of-the-art neural network architectures to real-world medical problems.

Course Topics
======

* Convolutional Neural Networks for Medical Imaging
* Time Series Analysis for Biomedical Signals
* Transformers in Medical AI
* Self-supervised and Contrastive Learning
* Long-tailed and Imbalanced Medical Data
* Clinical Decision Support Systems

Projects
======

Students complete hands-on projects involving:
* MRI image analysis for neurological disorders
* EEG signal processing for cognitive load assessment
* Development of AI models for clinical decision support

Assessment
======

* Research project (40%)
* Midterm examination (25%)  
* Final examination (25%)
* Class participation and assignments (10%)
