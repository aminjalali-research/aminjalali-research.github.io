Intensive workshop on applying artificial intelligence techniques to medical image analysis, focusing on practical implementation and real-world applications in clinical settings.

Workshop Content
======

* Introduction to Medical AI
* Preprocessing techniques for medical images
* CNN architectures for medical imaging
* Transfer learning and domain adaptation
* Evaluation metrics for medical AI systems

Hands-on Sessions
======

* Implementing medical image classification models
* Working with MRI and CT scan data
* Building end-to-end AI pipelines for clinical use
* Model validation and clinical deployment considerations
